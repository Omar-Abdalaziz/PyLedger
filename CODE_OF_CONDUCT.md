# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in PyLedger a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Welcome:** empathy, constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, grace toward mistakes.
**Unacceptable:** harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information, sexualized language or imagery, any conduct unprofessional in a shared project.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, chats) and in public when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (contact section). Maintainers will review, may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove contributors for serious or repeated violations, and will respect reporter confidentiality.
